Ohio beverage industry

Find the right distributor for your Ohio business

BevQuery helps Ohio wine shops, beverage retailers, restaurants and bars identify distributors by brand and county.

Answer product requests without calling multiple distributors

When a customer asks for a particular beer or wine, the brand name may be all you have. BevQuery lets you start with a partial brand or producer name, choose a matching listing and see distributor representation in your county.

What if the brand is carried elsewhere in Ohio?

Search results distinguish distributors in your county from distributors representing the brand elsewhere in Ohio. A statewide match is not a guarantee of availability in your county; confirm details with the distributor.

Ask about pricing and availability

Add identifying details or a product question to your search. Queries containing additional information are routed to participating distributors, who can respond through BevQuery. A search without a note is not routed.

Search by beverage type

Learn more about wine distributor lookup and beer distributor lookup. BevQuery is intended for beverage industry businesses, not consumer ordering.
