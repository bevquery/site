Ohio beverage industry

Find wine distributors by brand in Ohio

Identify wine distributor representation by producer or brand and Ohio county using BevQuery.

Start with the winery, producer or brand

A wine label may include a producer, vineyard, region and vintage. When looking for distributor representation, start with the brand or producer name rather than assuming the full label is the registered brand. BevQuery suggests matches as you type.

Check representation in your county

Different distributors may represent a wine brand in different parts of Ohio. BevQuery separates matches in your county from other Ohio representation, helping you identify a starting point for a product inquiry.

Confirm the specific wine

A brand match does not establish that a particular vintage, varietal or bottle size is in stock. Add those details to your query if you want participating distributors to respond about availability or pricing.
